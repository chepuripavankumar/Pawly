1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Adoption applications: your contact details, home type, other pets, household information and anything you write to the shelter.
  • Orders and subscriptions: shipping address, items purchased, delivery preferences and payment confirmation (card details are handled by our payment processor, not stored by Pawly).
  • Service bookings: your pet's name, type, notes and the appointment you requested.
  • Seller information: business name, contact details, listings and payout details.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, searches, device and browser type, and approximate location from your IP address.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the Platform — process orders and subscriptions, send applications to shelters and booking requests to providers.
  • To send confirmations, receipts, reminders (such as vaccination reminders you opt into) and customer support messages.
  • To personalize recommendations, such as pets, products or services near you.
  • To keep the Platform safe, prevent fraud and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • With your consent, to send newsletters and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the shelter or rescue you apply to, so it can review your application;
  • with the provider you book with, and the seller who fulfills your order;
  • with trusted service providers who help us operate (hosting, payments, email, analytics), under contracts that protect your data;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of Pawly, our users or animals;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you.

4. Location & GPS data

If you use a GPS dog belt or tracker bought on Pawly, your pet's location data is collected and processed by the device maker through its own app, under its own privacy policy. Pawly does not receive live location data from these devices. If you let us use your device location, we use it only to show nearby pets, shelters and services.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your cart and favorites, and understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work without them.

6. Retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, tax records for orders). We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular security reviews, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Access, correct or delete your personal information from your account or by contacting us.
  • Opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Depending on where you live — for example, California residents under the CCPA — you may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

8. Children

Pawly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Adoption applications, purchases and bookings require users to be 18 or older.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you by email or on the Platform.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Use our contact form or email privacy@pawly.example.
