Our service model

How Pawly services work

Pawly is an online-first startup. We don't own salons, clinics or vans. You book on Pawly, and a vetted local partner business does the job, either at your home or at their place.

🚐

1. We come to you

Mobile grooming vans, house-call vets, in-home pet sitters and trainers come to your address. Look for the Comes to your home tag and enter your address when you book.

📍

2. You visit the provider

Grooming salons, vet and vaccine clinics, daycare centers and boarding hotels. You drop your pet off at the partner's location at the time you booked.

🤝

3. Done by partner businesses

Every job is carried out by a licensed, insured partner that has passed our review. You book and pay on Pawly. The partner is paid weekly, and Pawly keeps a 6 to 10% commission per booking.
