🐾 About Pawly

One home for everything pets.

Pet owners juggle separate websites for adoption, food, supplies, grooming, daycare and vet visits. Pawly brings them together in one simple platform — and helps local shelters and pet businesses reach the people who need them.

Does Pawly sell pets?

No. Pawly never sells animals. Adoptable pets are listed by partner shelters and rescues, who review applications and complete adoptions.

How do food subscriptions work?

Choose "Subscribe & save" on any food product to get 10% off and automatic delivery every 2, 4 or 6 weeks. Skip or cancel anytime.

How do I cancel an appointment?

Go to My Favorites & Bookings. Cancellations are free up to 24 hours before the appointment.
