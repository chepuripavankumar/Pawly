Quick checklist

Healthy pet basics

🩺

Yearly checkups

An annual wellness exam (twice a year for seniors) catches problems early.

💉

Vaccines on time

Follow your vet's schedule for core and lifestyle vaccines. See schedule

🦷

Dental care

Brush a few times a week and use dental chews to prevent gum disease.

🐛

Parasite prevention

Monthly flea, tick and heartworm prevention protects pets year-round.

🏷️

ID & microchip

Keep tags and microchip details current. A GPS belt adds real-time peace of mind.

⚖️

Healthy weight

Measure meals and adjust for activity — you should feel ribs without seeing them.
