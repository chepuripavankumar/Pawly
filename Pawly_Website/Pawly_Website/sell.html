Seller FAQ

Questions from businesses

Who can sell on Pawly?

Registered businesses and independent makers selling pet food, supplies and accessories, plus licensed service providers such as groomers, daycares, trainers and veterinary clinics.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts are sent weekly to your bank account for completed orders and appointments, minus the commission for your plan.

Can I sell or rehome animals?

No. Pawly does not allow the sale of live animals. Adoption listings come only from our partner shelters and rescue organizations.

Do I need insurance or a license?

Service providers must hold any licenses required in their state (for example, veterinary licensing) and should carry appropriate business insurance.
